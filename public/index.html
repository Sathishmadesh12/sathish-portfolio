MERN or PERN: how I choose

MERN · MongoDB

Best when data is document-shaped or changes often, like product catalogs with flexible attributes. I used it for Aeroservice, with indexes tuned for fast catalog search.

PERN · PostgreSQL

Best when data has strict relationships and reporting needs, like fees, attendance or tenants. I used it for Edubotix and Khayash.

Full stack FAQ

Questions clients ask

What does a full stack developer do on your projects?

I own the whole path: database schema, API, React interface and deployment. One person making these decisions together means fewer handoffs and fewer mismatches between front and back end.

When do you pick PostgreSQL over MongoDB?

PostgreSQL when records relate to each other and need reports, such as students, fees and attendance. MongoDB when the shape of the data varies, such as a catalog with many product types.

How do you keep one customer's data apart from another's?

On Khayash, each organization gets schema-level isolation in PostgreSQL, on top of role-based access and AES encryption. It has had zero cross-tenant incidents.

How do you secure an API?

JWT and OAuth 2.0 for authentication, role-based access for permissions, and encryption for sensitive fields. I add these from the first endpoint, not at the end.
